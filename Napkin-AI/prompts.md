# Prompts Used

## Prompt 1 – Article Generation

Write a well-structured 500-word article on "Future Skills for Students" covering the importance of digital literacy, artificial intelligence, critical thinking, communication, collaboration, adaptability, creativity, leadership, and lifelong learning. The article should be suitable for college students and easy to convert into visual diagrams and infographics.

---

## Prompt 2 – Infographic

Convert the article into an infographic highlighting the most important future skills students need to succeed in the AI-driven world.

---

## Prompt 3 – Process Diagram

Create a process diagram showing the step-by-step journey of a student developing future-ready skills from learning to career success.

---

## Prompt 4 – Mind Map

Create a mind map based on the article with "Future Skills for Students" as the central theme and all related skills as branches.

---

## Prompt 5 – Social Media Visual

Create an engaging social media visual summarizing the top future skills every student should develop before graduation.
