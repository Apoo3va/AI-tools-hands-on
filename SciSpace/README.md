# 📚 SciSpace – Research Paper Simplification Project

## 🎯 Objective
This project demonstrates how AI-powered research tools like SciSpace can help simplify complex academic papers into structured, understandable insights. The goal was to improve comprehension speed while maintaining clarity and academic depth.

---

## 🛠 Tool Used
- SciSpace AI Research Assistant

---

## 📌 What This Project Involves
A single research paper was selected and analyzed using SciSpace to extract meaningful insights in a simplified format. The focus was on understanding the paper beyond technical jargon and breaking it into digestible sections.

---

## 📄 Project Workflow

1. Selected a published research paper from a credible academic source  
2. Uploaded it into SciSpace for analysis  
3. Extracted key components:
   - Simple explanation of the paper  
   - Step-by-step methodology  
   - Main contributions  
   - Limitations  
   - Future improvements  
4. Created a visual flow representation of the research process  

---

## 📦 Deliverables

- 📄 Original Research Paper (PDF)
- 🧠 Simplified Explanation (human-readable summary)
- ⚙️ Methodology Breakdown (step-by-step format)
- ⚠️ Limitations of the Study
- 🚀 Future Scope Suggestions
- 📊 Visual Flow Diagram (Input → Output structure)

---

## 💡 Outcome
This exercise helped bridge the gap between academic research and practical understanding by converting complex technical content into simple, structured insights.

---

## 🚀 Key Learning
- Breaking down research papers into structured components  
- Understanding AI-assisted reading and summarization  
- Improving speed of academic comprehension without losing meaning
