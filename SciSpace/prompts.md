# 🤖 SciSpace Prompting Guide – Research Paper Analysis

This document contains the exact prompts used in SciSpace to extract structured insights from a research paper.

Each prompt was designed to simplify complex academic content into clear, usable information.

---

## 📌 1. Overall Understanding
> Explain this research paper in simple and clear language.  
> Summarize the main problem, approach, and results in an easy-to-understand way.

---

## 📌 2. Beginner-Friendly Explanation
> Explain this paper in a way that a beginner can understand.  
> Avoid technical jargon and use simple examples if possible.

---

## 📌 3. Methodology Breakdown
> Break down the methodology of this paper step by step including:
> - Dataset used  
> - Data preprocessing steps  
> - Model or algorithm used  
> - Training process  
> - Evaluation metrics  

---

## 📌 4. Core Idea / Contribution
> What is the main idea or contribution of this research paper?  
> Explain it clearly in simple terms.

---

## 📌 5. Limitations
> Identify the limitations or weaknesses of this research paper.  
> Focus on practical and real-world constraints.

---

## 📌 6. Future Improvements
> Suggest possible future improvements or research directions based on this paper.

---

## 📌 7. Visual Representation
> Provide a simple flow of the research process:
> Input → Processing → Model → Output → Result
