# Smart College Management System – Explanation

The Smart College Management System is a centralized digital platform designed to automate and manage various academic and administrative activities within a college. The system streamlines processes such as student registration, course enrollment, attendance monitoring, examination management, result generation, and report access.

The flowchart illustrates the overall workflow followed by students throughout an academic cycle. It begins with student login and progresses through registration, attendance tracking, examinations, and result generation.

The user journey diagram highlights the experience of a student interacting with the system. It demonstrates how users navigate through various services provided by the platform.

The class diagram represents the structural design of the system. It defines the key entities such as Student, Faculty, Course, Attendance, Examination, Result, and Admin, along with their relationships and responsibilities.

The sequence diagram explains the interaction between system components during course enrollment. It shows how requests flow between the student, portal, database, and administrator.

Overall, the Smart College Management System improves efficiency, reduces manual effort, enhances transparency, and provides real-time access to academic information for students, faculty, and administrators.
