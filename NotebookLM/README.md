# NotebookLM – AI Research Assistant

## Assignment Title

Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

## Objective

The objective of this assignment is to explore NotebookLM as an AI-powered research assistant by analyzing multiple sources related to Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare. The tool was used to organize information, generate study materials, create assessments, and develop a structured learning resource based on uploaded documents.

## Sources Used

* 3 Research Papers on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
* 2 Articles related to AI applications and trends in Healthcare
* 1 YouTube Transcript on AI in Healthcare

## Activities Performed

The uploaded sources were analyzed using NotebookLM to generate:

* Study Notes
* Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* Slide Deck Outline
* 20 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
* Audio Overview Summary
* Learning Guide

## Deliverables

* Study Notes
* FAQ Document
* Slide Deck
* 20 MCQs with Answers
* Audio Overview
* Learning Guide
* Research Portfolio PDF

## Key Learnings

* Understanding the role of AI in modern healthcare systems
* Applications of machine learning and deep learning in medical diagnosis
* Benefits of AI for patient care and hospital management
* Challenges such as data privacy, ethics, and implementation barriers
* Future opportunities of AI-driven healthcare solutions

## Skills Demonstrated

* Research and Information Synthesis
* AI-Assisted Learning
* Technical Documentation
* Critical Analysis
* Educational Content Creation
* Knowledge Organization

NotebookLM

NotebookLM successfully transformed multiple research sources into structured learning materials, demonstrating its effectiveness as an AI-powered research and study assistant.
