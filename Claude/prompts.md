# Prompts Used

## Prompt 1 – Professional Report

You are an expert education policy researcher, technology analyst, and professional report writer.

Prepare a comprehensive professional report on:

"Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Education in India"

Requirements:

* Approximately 1500 words
* Executive Summary
* Introduction
* Current State of Education in India
* AI Applications in Education
* Benefits and Challenges
* Ethical Considerations
* Future Trends
* SWOT Analysis
* Recommendations
* Conclusion
* References

Include India-specific examples, government initiatives, and EdTech platforms.

---

## Prompt 2 – School Students Version

Rewrite the entire report for school students aged 13–18 years.

Requirements:

* Simple language
* Easy examples
* Beginner-friendly explanations
* 800–1000 words

---

## Prompt 3 – College Students Version

Rewrite the report for college students.

Requirements:

* Moderate technical depth
* Focus on careers, skills, research, and higher education
* 1000–1200 words

---

## Prompt 4 – Policymakers Version

Rewrite the report for policymakers and government stakeholders.

Requirements:

* Formal policy-oriented tone
* Governance and regulation focus
* Actionable recommendations
* Long-term educational transformation perspective
