# 📊 AI Awareness Survey – Summary Report

## 📌 Objective
To analyze the awareness and adoption of AI tools among college students.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- Majority of students are aware of AI tools like ChatGPT and similar platforms  
- Around 70–80% students use AI for assignments or learning support  
- Most students believe AI improves productivity and understanding  
- Moderate trust level in AI-generated content  
- Strong interest in learning AI-related skills
