
# 🧠 Reflection – AI Awareness Survey (Google Forms)

## 📌 Overview
This assignment helped in understanding how surveys can be used to collect meaningful insights about technology adoption among students.

---

## 📌 What I Learned
- How to design structured survey questions  
- Importance of mixing MCQs, rating scales, and open-ended responses  
- How data visualization helps in understanding trends  
- Real-world use of surveys in research and analytics  

---

## 📊 Insights Gained
- AI usage among students is already high  
- Students are comfortable using AI for academic tasks  
- Trust and ethical concerns still exist  
- There is strong interest in learning AI skills  

---

## ⏱ Time Efficiency
Manually collecting and analyzing survey data would take several hours.  
Using Google Forms and automated charts reduced analysis time significantly.

---

## 🚀 Final Takeaway
Surveys are a powerful tool for understanding user behavior, and AI tools can further enhance data analysis and visualization efficiency.
