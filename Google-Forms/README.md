# 📊 Google Forms – AI Awareness Survey

## 🎯 Objective
This project aims to study the awareness, understanding, and adoption of Artificial Intelligence among college students using a structured survey.

---

## 🛠 Tool Used
- Google Forms

---

## 📌 Project Overview
A survey was designed with 15 carefully structured questions to analyze:
- Awareness of AI tools
- Usage patterns among students
- Perception of AI in education
- Future expectations of AI adoption

---

## 🧩 Survey Structure
The form includes:
- Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
- Rating Scale Questions (1–5 Likert Scale)
- Open-ended Questions

---

## 📦 Deliverables
- Google Form (Survey Link)
- Response Collection Sheet (Google Sheets)
- Summary Report
- Data Visualizations (Charts)

---

## 💡 Outcome
This survey provides insights into how students interact with AI tools and how AI is shaping modern learning environments.
