# Prompts Used

1. I am an ECE undergraduate student interested in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Create a detailed 3-year roadmap to become an AI/ML Engineer including skills, projects, internships, certifications, and career milestones.

2. Break the AI/ML Engineer roadmap into semester-wise goals for the next 3 years considering I'm 1st year student.

3.List all technical skills required to become an AI/ML Engineer and categorize them into beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.

4. Suggest a complete learning path for Python, Data Structures and Algorithms, Mathematics, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and MLOps.

5. Recommend the best free and paid learning resources, courses, books, YouTube channels, and websites for becoming an AI/ML Engineer.

6.Suggest 15 AI/ML projects that can strengthen my GitHub portfolio, starting from beginner level and progressing to advanced level.

7. What certifications are most valuable for AI/ML Engineer roles and in what order should I complete them?

8. Create a month-by-month action plan for the next 12 months to help me prepare for AI internships and placement opportunities.

9. Review an AI/ML fresher resume and suggest improvements that would make it more attractive to recruiters.

10.What interview preparation strategy should I follow for AI/ML Engineer internships including coding, machine learning concepts, projects, and behavioral interviews?
