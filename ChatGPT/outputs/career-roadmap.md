# 3-Year Roadmap to Become an AI/ML Engineer (ECE Student)

## Career Goal

**AI/ML Engineer**

An AI/ML Engineer develops machine learning models, deploys AI solutions, builds intelligent systems, and works on data-driven applications across industries such as healthcare, finance, automotive, robotics, and telecommunications.

# Expected Outcome After 3 Years

By following this roadmap consistently, you should graduate with:

* Strong Python and ML fundamentals
* 12–15 high-quality projects
* 1–2 internships
* Knowledge of Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, and Generative AI
* Deployment and MLOps experience
* Competitive resume for AI/ML Engineer roles
* Readiness for placements at companies such as Google, Microsoft, Amazon, NVIDIA, and AI-focused startups.
