# 🚀 Genspark AI – Market Research & Business Proposal

## 🎯 Objective
This project demonstrates the use of AI agent-based tools to perform end-to-end business research, analysis, and presentation creation using a single prompt workflow.

The goal was to act as a consultant for a college startup incubator and design a complete business proposal for:

> **“Launching an AI Skill Development Center for College Students”**

---

## 🧠 Tool Used
- Genspark AI (Agent-based AI workspace)

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project includes:
- Market Research Report  
- Competitor Analysis  
- SWOT Analysis  
- Business Model Canvas  
- Marketing Strategy  
- Budget Estimation  
- Revenue Model  
- Executive Summary  
- 10-slide Investor Presentation  

---

## 📦 Deliverables
- Research Report (PDF)  
- Pitch Deck (10 slides)  
- Prompt Used  
- Screenshots of AI outputs  
- Reflection Report  

---

## 💡 Outcome
This project shows how AI agents can replace multiple stages of business consulting work by automating research, analysis, and presentation generation in a unified workflow.
